About

I am a final year Computer Science Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, my work mainly revolves around VLMs, LLMs and SLMs. I also enjoy working with RAG, agents and retrieval infrastructure.

Most of what I build sits on the server side: schedulers, workers, queues, databases and the APIs in front of them. I like problems where the obvious implementation is the one that quietly loses data at 3am, and the job is to notice that before a user does.

The AI work is the same discipline applied to a different component. I build retrieval pipelines and orchestration with Python, LangChain, LangGraph and FAISS, and I spend most of my effort on the parts that are not the model: whether it returns the right context, whether the output parses, and what it costs per request.

Outside of work I solve competitive programming problems and previously led research projects at Logic Play Club.

ReachInbox Live Private repo

A bulk email scheduling engine with durable delivery guarantees. A campaign is written to PostgreSQL in one transaction, then each recipient becomes its own retryable job. A crash never loses work and never resends.

  • Transactional outbox pattern keeps side effects such as search indexing and Slack alerts from being lost.
  • Atomic claim with lease expiry and startup reconciliation every 60 seconds, so restarts recover cleanly.
  • Atomic Redis Lua rate limiting on whole-hour windows, with the slot returned when a send fails.
  • 75 unit and integration tests, 29 Playwright browser tests, plus failure injection and outage suites.
