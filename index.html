About

I am a Computer Science Engineering student at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, working as a Back-End AI Engineering Intern at FlyRank AI where I ship LLM powered services into production workflows.

My interest is in the part of software that has to keep working when things go wrong. That means designing around failure rather than hoping it does not happen: atomic job claiming, transactional outboxes, leases that expire, queues treated as a notification system rather than a source of truth.

On the AI side I work on retrieval pipelines and orchestration, mostly with Python, LangChain, LangGraph and FAISS. I care about the boring engineering around the model: latency, structured output that does not break, evaluation, and retrieval that actually returns relevant context.

Outside of that I solve competitive programming problems and lead the research group at Logic Play Club.

ReachInbox Live Private repo

A bulk email scheduling engine with durable delivery guarantees. A campaign is written to PostgreSQL in one transaction, then each recipient becomes its own retryable job. A crash never loses work and never resends.

  • Transactional outbox pattern keeps side effects such as search indexing and Slack alerts from being lost.
  • Atomic claim with lease expiry and startup reconciliation every 60 seconds, so restarts recover cleanly.
  • Atomic Redis Lua rate limiting on whole-hour windows, with the slot returned when a send fails.
  • 75 unit and integration tests, 29 Playwright browser tests, plus failure injection and outage suites.
